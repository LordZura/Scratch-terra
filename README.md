# How to play:

basic controls: 
- WASD: movement
- oh, nothing else.

main objective: 
move to the yellow circle which is like a finish line, and on your way try to evade the enemies

how enemies work:
entire game is a grid, so they move towards you the way they can, BUT they also have random choice of direction in specific places.
the second enemy, appears after round 5, and another one of that after 10, a darker shade of blue, it skips one cell, it might be good for you, or bad, he can catch up really fast, you need to think before you move!

thats it. play, beat highscores.
